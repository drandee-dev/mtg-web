Can I counter a triggered ability?
MTG Workshop AI

Yes — triggered abilities can be countered with effects that specifically target abilities, like Stifle or Disallow. Standard "counter target spell" won't work.

Your window: after the trigger goes on the stack, players get priority before it resolves.

Ask about interactions, triggered abilities, priority, or anything else in plain English. Answers cite the Comprehensive Rules.

Can I counter a triggered ability?
MTG Workshop AI

Yes — triggered abilities can be countered, but you need an effect that specifically targets abilities, not just spells. Cards like Stifle, Disallow, and Repudiate work. A standard "counter target spell" effect won't — triggered abilities aren't spells once they're on the stack.

The timing window: after a triggered ability's condition is met, it goes on the stack when a player would receive priority. Players then respond in APNAP order — that's your opportunity to counter it before it resolves.
